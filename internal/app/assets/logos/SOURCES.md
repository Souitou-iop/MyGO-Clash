# Logo sources

The logos here identify the sites and services the connectivity page tests.
They are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify them.

- Most vectors: Logos by lndev (github.com/ln-dev7/logos-apps), MIT for the
  collection; see LICENSE.
- Single-colour marks (airtel, codecademy, coinmarketcap, expedia, indigo,
  linear, mlb, monzo, nba, nytimes, paytm, playstation, revolut, roblox,
  visa) and duckduckgo: Simple Icons (simpleicons.org), CC0-1.0.
- microsoft: the four squares, drawn here.
- The PNGs (abema and tvb-anywhere among them), and the SVGs of banorte, e-albania, kayak, musixmatch and
  raiffeisen-ks: each site's own icon, as the site serves it.

Sites without a logo here show their initial.
