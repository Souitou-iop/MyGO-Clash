# Logo sources

The logos here identify the sites and services the connectivity page tests.
They are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify them.

- Most vectors: Logos by lndev (github.com/ln-dev7/logos-apps), MIT for the
  collection; see LICENSE.
- Single-colour marks (airtel, codecademy, coinmarketcap, expedia, indigo,
  linear, mlb, monzo, nba, nytimes, paytm, playstation, revolut, roblox,
  visa) and duckduckgo, cashapp, channel4, elevenlabs, jal, namuwiki,
  paramount, singaporeair, tradingview, united: Simple Icons
  (simpleicons.org), CC0-1.0.
- Logos of one brand under several ids are copies, as the lookup is by id:
  anthropic-api (claude), openai-api (chatgpt), gemini-api (gemini),
  docker-registry (dockerhub), ghcr and github-raw (github), gcr (gcloud),
  cloudflare-dns (cloudflare), google-dns and google-maps (google),
  steamcommunity (steam), psn (playstation), shopee-tw (shopee),
  naver-webtoon (naver).
- microsoft: the four squares, drawn here.
- The PNGs (abema and tvb-anywhere among them), and the SVGs of banorte, e-albania, kayak, musixmatch and
  raiffeisen-ks: each site's own icon, as the site serves it. So are the
  PNGs and SVGs of the Japan, Korea, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, USA and
  UK groups and of the later additions without a lndev logo (civitai,
  aistudio, pluto, viki, iqiyi-intl, wetv, kkbox, joox, hoyoverse, garena,
  moomoo, etherscan, defillama, dhgate, asos, economist, voa-zh, initium,
  zaobao, foxnews, cathaypacific, hostelworld, airalo, transfermarkt,
  fotmob, goal, and the brands of those groups); .ico files are converted
  to PNG.

Sites without a logo here show their initial.
